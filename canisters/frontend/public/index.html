Event Horizon

Best-effort wake-ups for ICP-aware canisters, without making notification delivery part of their correctness path.

Subscription memo

Endow at least 10 ICP through Jupiter Faucet. Leave the trigger amount blank to react to every incoming transfer, or set an inclusive minimum.

Fast path

Event Horizon reads the ICP Ledger directly and calls poke(subaccounts) after a poll sees relevant activity.

Correctness stays yours

Your canister keeps its own authoritative Ledger/Index cursor and a slower independent reconciliation timer.

It can beat the Index

A poke may arrive before the ICP Index exposes the new transaction. Your application chooses its own retry/backoff policy.

Best effort by design

Only matched subaccount numbers are passed; there are no transaction payloads, acknowledgements, retry queue or delivery guarantee. Missed pokes affect responsiveness, not completeness.

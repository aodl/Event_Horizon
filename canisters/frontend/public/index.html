Jupiter Faucet endowments fund Event Horizon and are ultimately converted into cycles. A higher ICP/XDR rate creates more cycles per endowed ICP, so fewer ICP are needed. Event Horizon conservatively compares the latest successful daily CMC observation with the lowest daily observation it successfully recorded during the trailing 1,461 UTC-day window. It samples roughly daily, accepts missed samples, rounds upward to whole ICP, and changes prices only at monthly UTC boundaries.
